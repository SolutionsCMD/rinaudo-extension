Privacy Policy

Mizkif Global (browser extension) · Last updated 8 August 2026

This policy explains what the Mizkif Global browser extension ("the extension") does with your data. It applies only to the extension, not to the rinaudoglobal.com website or any other service.

What the extension does

The extension lets you earn tickets in Mizkif's game by engaging with his content: liking, commenting on, watching, and reposting his promoted posts and videos on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and X (the site at x.com, which also answers to twitter.com), and voting on live polls from his Kick stream. It also notifies you when he goes live or posts. Tickets are credited to the account you link once with "Connect with Kick."

Which sites it runs on

The extension loads on these sites and nowhere else:

  • kick.com, on Mizkif's channel page: to show the live poll and vote card.
  • youtube.com and m.youtube.com: to spot your like, comment, or watch on one of his promoted videos.
  • tiktok.com and m.tiktok.com: the same, for his promoted posts.
  • instagram.com (and instagr.am): the same, for his promoted posts.
  • x.com and twitter.com: to spot your like, reply, or repost on one of his promoted posts. The next section covers exactly how the repost check works.
  • s2.jsolutions.dev/extension/connect: the single page where "Connect with Kick" finishes and hands the extension your account token.

On every other site the extension is not loaded at all, so it cannot see the page.

How the repost check works on X

A repost earns tickets only when X itself confirms the repost happened. A button click on its own is not proof, so on x.com and twitter.com the extension watches the requests the X page sends while you use it. Here is the whole of what that means:

  • It checks the web address of each request. Nearly every request X makes is left alone at that point and never looked at again.
  • Only when the address is X's own "create repost" call does the extension look any further, and the only thing it takes from that request is the id of the post you reposted.
  • It notes whether X accepted the request. That is a yes or no, not content.
  • It never reads what X sends back. The reply is handed straight to the page untouched: the extension does not open it, change it, delay it, or block it.

It does not read your timeline, your posts, your drafts, your direct messages, or your followers, and it does not read the words you type. This check runs on X only. It does not run on Kick, YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram, and it does not run on any other site.

What we store

  • Your account token. When you choose "Connect with Kick," a single authentication token is stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage.local). It is used only to authenticate your requests to s2.jsolutions.dev, never leaves your browser except in the Authorization header of calls to that server, and is removed when you disconnect or uninstall.
  • An anti-fraud device token. A random identifier the extension generates the first time it runs and stores locally (chrome.storage.local). It is not derived from your hardware and contains no personal information. It simply lets the game tell one browser install apart from another so a single person can't farm tickets across many accounts. You can reset it by clearing the extension's storage or reinstalling.
  • A device fingerprint. The extension computes a one-way hash of general device characteristics (graphics renderer, a canvas drawing, browser/timezone/CPU details) and caches that hash locally. We store only the resulting hash, never the underlying values, and use it only to detect duplicate accounts and automated abuse.
  • Local state only. The extension also keeps small bookkeeping values locally (e.g. the last poll/video/post already shown, so you aren't notified twice, and where you dragged the on-page card). These stay on your device.

What we send, and to whom

The extension communicates with only two servers:

  • https://s2.jsolutions.dev (the game API): to exchange your Connect-with-Kick login for an account token; to record when you like, comment on, watch, or repost one of Mizkif's promoted posts or videos; to fetch the active poll and submit your vote; to fetch the public reward-rate list; and, when a reply on X does not register, a short troubleshooting note (which button was clicked and how many characters your draft was, never the words themselves) so the bug can be found and fixed.
  • https://rinaudoglobal.com (public status feed): the extension periodically checks whether Mizkif is live or has posted, to power notifications. This requires no login and sends none of your data.

When an engagement is credited, three things go out: which site and which post (its id, the same one you can read in the post's public web address), which action it was (like, comment, watch, or repost), and your account token, so the tickets land on your account. While a video plays, the extension also sends periodic "still watching" signals. Every one of these requests carries the anti-fraud device token and fingerprint described in the next section.

What never leaves your browser: the contents of the pages you visit, the text of your comments (we send only that you commented on a given post, never what you wrote), your direct messages, your passwords, your browsing history, and anything at all from a site the extension does not run on.

Preventing duplicate accounts and bots

Because tickets have real value, we take basic steps to stop one person from farming them with many fake accounts or bots. With each authenticated request to s2.jsolutions.dev, the extension includes the anti-fraud device token and device fingerprint described above. The server additionally records a one-way, salted hash of your IP address (your actual IP address is never stored, only a hash that can be compared for a match, not reversed). These signals are used only to group accounts that appear to share a device or network for manual review before payouts. They are never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to track your browsing, and they are never shown to other users.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or behavioral tracking of how you use the web. The only device and network signals we collect are the anti-fraud identifiers described above, used solely to keep the ticket economy fair.
  • No advertising, and no selling or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No tracking of your general browsing. The extension runs only on the sites listed above, and only to detect your deliberate engagement with Mizkif's promoted posts and to show its own on-page card. It does not collect the contents of those pages. The one place it looks inside anything is the X repost check described above, which takes a post id out of a request the X page was already sending. The words you type in a comment stay on your device and are never transmitted; only the character count is, and only in the troubleshooting note described above.
  • No collection of names, emails, payment details, or other personal information by the extension itself.

Permissions, and why

  • storage: to store your account token and local state.
  • alarms: a periodic timer to check for open polls and new content.
  • notifications: desktop alerts when a poll opens or Mizkif goes live or posts.
  • host access to s2.jsolutions.dev and rinaudoglobal.com: to talk to the game API and the public notifications feed, and to finish the "Connect with Kick" sign-in.
  • host access to kick.com: to show the vote card on Mizkif's stream.
  • host access to youtube.com, tiktok.com, and instagram.com, including their mobile addresses: to detect and credit your likes, comments, and watches on Mizkif's promoted posts and videos there.
  • host access to x.com and twitter.com: to detect and credit your likes, replies, and reposts on his promoted posts there, including the repost check described above.

Open source

You do not have to take any of this on trust. The extension's code is public and you can read every line of it at github.com/SolutionsCMD/rinaudo-extension. The X repost check is the file content/observe.js.

Your choices

You can disconnect at any time from the extension popup, which deletes the stored token and tells the server to revoke it. Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data.
